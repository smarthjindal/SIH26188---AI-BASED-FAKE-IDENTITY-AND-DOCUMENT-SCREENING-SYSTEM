capabilities

Fourteen fraud vectors. One verdict.

Every scan is fused across OCR, MRZ math, computer vision, face-match and watchlist intelligence.

Document Forensics

ELA-based photo tampering, dot-pattern analysis, hologram signature & paper-texture verification.

MRZ / OCR Fusion

Compares printed data against MRZ checksum with ICAO 9303 validation to catch altered fields.

Live Face Match

1:1 face comparison with liveness detection — passenger vs. document photo vs. e-visa record.

Multi-Identity Detection

Cross-checks biometric graphs to surface aliases used at other checkpoints.

Watchlist Screening

Interpol Red Notice, MHA blacklist and SSB watchlists queried in real-time with fuzzy matching.

Edge-Ready

Runs offline at remote checkpoints, syncs verdicts when connectivity returns. No cloud dependency.
